What Crab Talk offers

Green, Boston and Australian Rock Lobster are not interchangeable. Green Lobster is versatile, Boston gives you claw meat, and Australian Rock Lobster has firmer meat and suits simpler cooking.

Green Lobster

A versatile live lobster for steaming, grilling, bee hoon and yee mee.

Boston Lobster

A familiar clawed lobster that suits steaming, butter, garlic and noodle dishes.

Australian Rock Lobster

A premium option with firm meat, suitable for simple cooking and special meals.

Choosing between lobster types

Green Lobster is often a practical value choice, Boston Lobster has a familiar clawed-lobster profile, and Australian Rock Lobster is a premium option. Actual size and condition still matter.

Cooking styles

  • Steam with garlic or ginger and scallion.
  • Grill or bake for a firmer, roasted profile.
  • Cook with bee hoon or yee mee for a sharing dish.
  • Ask Fai Kee separately about current cooking arrangements.
