Which cooked crab style should I choose?

Start with the sauce you enjoy, then choose whether you want crabs on their own or a family meal with sides. All four styles are available in the cooked-crab collection.

  • Chilli Crab: sweet, savoury and gently spicy; choose mantou if you enjoy dipping it in the sauce.
  • Black Pepper Crab: a bold, peppery option.
  • White Pepper Crab: a fragrant pepper option with a different character from black pepper.
  • Salted Egg Yolk Crab: the rich, savoury option.

The 2-crab and 4-pax bundles use one style for both crabs. To share two styles, choose the 4-crab-only bundle or the 8–10 pax feast: the split is exactly two crabs per style.

Chilli Crab delivery in Singapore

Choose Chilli Crab when you want the classic sweet, savoury and gently spicy Singapore-style crab experience. Crab Talk’s cooked bundles use live mud crabs selected from our tanks and cooked fresh to order, with free islandwide delivery on the core cooked-crab bundles.

Black Pepper Crab delivery in Singapore

Black Pepper Crab is available across the cooked-crab range for customers who prefer a bold, peppery sauce. The 2-crab and 4-pax bundles use one style for both crabs; selected four-crab bundles can be split into exactly two cooking styles.

White Pepper Crab delivery in Singapore

White Pepper Crab offers a fragrant pepper profile with a different character from the darker black-pepper sauce. It is available as a cooking-style option on the current cooked-crab bundles shown in the online store.

Salted Egg Yolk Crab delivery in Singapore

Salted Egg Yolk Crab is the rich, savoury option in Crab Talk’s four-style cooked-crab range. Choose it as the single flavour on a 2-crab or 4-pax bundle, or combine it with another eligible flavour on a four-crab bundle.

Crab-only bundles or a family feast?

The 2-crab bundle from S$168 and the 4-crab-only bundle from S$318 focus on the crabs. Both offer optional fried mantou: 6, 12, 18 or 24 pieces for S$6, S$12, S$18 or S$24 respectively.

The S$198 4-pax feast includes fried rice, 8 pieces of prawn paste chicken and a choice of 12 mantou or an omelette. The S$388 8–10 pax feast includes large fried rice, 16 pieces of prawn paste chicken, 18 mantou and a large omelette.

Crab weights are approximately 800–900g+- each before cooking. Portions depend on appetite and other dishes; use the bundle contents rather than crab count alone when planning your meal.

How cooked crab delivery works

  1. Choose the cooked crab bundle that fits your group.
  2. Select the available cooking style and any mantou option shown on the product.
  3. Select an available delivery slot online.
  4. Crab Talk arranges the cooked order for delivery to your Singapore address.

Important bundle rules

  • Cooked crab bundles are online only.
  • Customers cannot choose the individual crabs used in a cooked bundle.
  • The 2-crab and 4-pax bundles use one cooking style for both crabs.
  • Where a 4-crab bundle offers two cooking styles, it is exactly 2 crabs + 2 crabs.
  • Cooked-crab bundles include free islandwide delivery.
  • Place cooked-crab orders separately from regular seafood orders. The regular seafood S$180 free-delivery threshold does not apply to these cooked bundles.

Frequently asked questions

Does Crab Talk offer cooked crab delivery in Singapore?
