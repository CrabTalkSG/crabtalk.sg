Why choose Turbot

Turbot suits customers who want a premium fish different from the usual round fish. Its shape and meat structure make it a distinctive centrepiece.

Cooking advice

Keep the seasoning balanced. Steaming preserves delicacy, while roasting gives a firmer and more concentrated profile.
