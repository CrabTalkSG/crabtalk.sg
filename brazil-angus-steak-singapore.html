What Crab Talk offers

Both cuts come in 250g portions. Ribeye is the richer choice, while Striploin is firmer and a little leaner. At $12 per piece, they are also easy to add to a seafood dinner.

Brazil Angus Ribeye

Ribeye or Striploin

Choose Ribeye for a richer, more marbled steak. Choose Striploin for a firmer bite and a more defined lean-to-fat balance.
