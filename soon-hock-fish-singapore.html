Why Soon Hock is popular

The flesh is delicate and works best when cooked cleanly. Overcooking can reduce the premium texture.

Choosing a size

Choose the fish around the number of diners and the rest of the meal. If several dishes are being served, the largest Soon Hock is not automatically the best choice.

Preparation

Ask about scaling, cleaning and gutting. Cook as soon as practical after collection.

Frequently asked questions

Is Soon Hock the same as Marble Goby?

Yes. Soon Hock is the common Singapore name for Marble Goby.

What cooking style is recommended?

Hong Kong-style steaming is a popular choice because it keeps the flavour clean.

Can I reserve a particular size?

Yes, subject to current stock.
