What Crab Talk offers

The range is practical rather than huge: crabs and prawns for familiar Singapore dishes, shellfish for fast wok cooking, lobsters for sharing, and a small fish selection for steaming, soup or family meals.

Live Crabs

Mud, Yellow Roe, Red Roe, Double Shell and Alaskan King Crab.

Live Vannamei Prawns

Approx. 20 pieces per kg, featured at $38/kg.

Lobsters

Green, Boston and Australian Rock Lobster.

Oysters & Clams

Fanny Bay Sunseeker, Coffin Bay, seasonal oysters, Manila Clams and Razor Clams.

Fish

Soon Hock, Patin and Turbot.

Home-cooking extras

Kelong Sotong, Brazil Angus steaks, chilli crab sauce and mantou when available.

Why we keep the range manageable

A smaller, deliberate range makes it easier to explain each product properly, advise on cooking and arrange pre-orders instead of listing products that Crab Talk does not actively support.

How to check the day’s stock

  • Choose the product and quantity through the online store when it is listed.
  • State your preferred collection date and time.
  • For live seafood, mention the planned cooking style and number of diners.

Preparation and collection

Crab Talk can discuss washing, cleaning, cutting and packing for suitable products. Premium or seasonal items should be reserved before travelling down.
