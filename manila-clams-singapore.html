What Crab Talk offers

Manila Clams are one of the easiest shellfish to cook at home. They open quickly, release a sweet broth and work with anything from garlic and chilli to soup, noodles or pasta.

Manila Clams

Fresh shellfish for home cooking and sharing dishes.

Garlic or Chilli

Fast, flavourful stir-fry styles.

Soup or Noodles

Adds shellfish sweetness to broth and noodles.

Preparation tips

Rinse and clean according to the condition of the clams. Discard shells that remain open before cooking or do not open after proper cooking.

Cooking ideas

  • Steam with ginger, scallion or Chinese wine-style aromatics.
  • Stir-fry with garlic, chilli or black bean.
  • Add to soup, bee hoon, pasta or seafood broth.

Buying advice

Confirm the current quantity and collection time. Clams are best cooked promptly after collection.

Frequently asked questions
