What Crab Talk offers

The whole point of Yellow Roe Crab is the roe. When the batch is good, the best cooking styles are the ones that keep the roe intact, visible and easy to taste.

Sri Lanka Yellow Roe Crab

A premium roe option when strong stock is available.

Cold Crab

A clean preparation that highlights the roe and natural sweetness.

Grilled or Steamed

Simple heat keeps the roe prominent without heavy sauce.

What makes a good Yellow Roe Crab

The roe should look full and appealing, while the crab should still have firm body condition. Bigger is not automatically better; the actual roe quality matters.

Recommended cooking approach

Avoid overpowering premium roe with a very heavy sauce. Cold crab, gentle grilling or simple steaming usually lets the roe remain the reason you paid more.

Price and availability

Premium roe crab pricing changes with origin, season, size and roe condition. Check the current product listing rather than relying on an old price.

Frequently asked questions

Is Yellow Roe Crab the same as Red Roe Crab?

No. They are different roe presentations and can vary in appearance, texture and flavour.

Is Yellow Roe Crab good for chilli crab?

It can be cooked that way, but a simpler style usually gives better value when the roe quality is the main attraction.

Can I reserve Yellow Roe Crab?

Yes, subject to the current batch. Reservation is recommended.
