Before you visit

Common questions.

Stock can change quickly, so a short WhatsApp message is the safest way to avoid a wasted trip.

What products are available every day?

Availability varies. Crab Talk focuses on the range shown on this website, but exact sizes, origins and quantities depend on the latest arrival.

Can I reserve seafood?

Yes. Reservations are recommended for live prawns, lobsters, premium crabs, oysters, fish and larger quantities.

Do you sell by weight or by piece?

It depends on the product. Some seafood is sold by weight, while selected crabs or packaged products may be priced by piece or box. Confirm before ordering.

Can you prepare the seafood?

Crab Talk can wash, clean, cut and pack suitable items. Tell us how you intend to cook it so the preparation can be discussed.
