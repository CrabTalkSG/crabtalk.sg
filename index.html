Before you visit

Common questions.

Stock can change quickly, so check the online store before making a special trip for a particular item.

What products are available every day?

Availability varies. The website shows the products we regularly carry, but exact sizes, origins and quantities depend on the latest arrival.

Can I reserve seafood?

Yes. Reservations are recommended for live prawns, lobsters, premium crabs, oysters, fish and larger quantities.

Do you sell by weight or by piece?

It depends on the product. Some seafood is sold by weight, while selected crabs or packaged products may be priced by piece or box. Confirm before ordering.

Can you prepare the seafood?

Crab Talk can wash, clean, cut and pack suitable items. Preparation depends on the seafood and how it will be cooked.
