How Red Roe differs

Red Roe and Yellow Roe can differ in colour, texture and flavour. The best choice is personal, so compare the actual batch rather than assuming one is automatically superior.

How to get better value

Choose based on roe fullness, body condition and planned cooking style. Heavy sauce can hide the quality you are paying for.

Reservation advice

Red Roe Crab is not guaranteed every day. Check the online store for the current listing before planning around a particular quantity.

Frequently asked questions

Which is better, Red Roe or Yellow Roe Crab?

There is no universal winner. The better choice depends on the current batch and your preferred roe texture and flavour.

How should Red Roe Crab be cooked?

Steaming, grilling or a cold preparation are practical ways to keep the roe prominent.

Is it sold every day?

No. Availability depends on arrivals and should be checked before visiting.
