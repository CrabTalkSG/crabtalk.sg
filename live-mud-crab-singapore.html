What Crab Talk offers

Mud Crab is the workhorse of the crab counter. It has the meat and shell strength to handle chilli crab or black pepper, but a good crab is just as satisfying steamed with very little seasoning.

Best cooking styles

Mud Crab is especially suitable for chilli crab, black pepper crab, ginger and scallion, steaming and bee hoon. The ideal size depends on the number of diners and whether it is the main dish.

What to check before buying

  • Active movement and firm legs.
  • Good shell condition and appropriate weight for its size.
  • A size that matches your cooking pot, wok and dining group.

Why reserve first

Live crab supply changes quickly, particularly for popular sizes. The online store is the quickest way to see which pickup options are currently listed.
