What Crab Talk offers

Har Lok

Steamed or Barbecued

Why the size matters

Approximately 20 prawns per kilogram means each prawn is large enough to feel substantial on the plate. Actual count can vary slightly by batch.

How to order

Tell Crab Talk how many kilograms you need and when you plan to collect. Pre-ordering helps us arrange additional supply instead of relying only on the quantity currently at the stall.

Cooking ideas

  • Steam with garlic or Chinese wine-style aromatics.
  • Cook har lok with a savoury-sweet sauce.
  • Barbecue or grill for a simple gathering dish.
  • Add to San Lou Hor Fun, bee hoon or seafood noodles.
