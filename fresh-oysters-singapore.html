How to choose oysters

Check the origin, size grade, shell condition and how you plan to serve them. Larger is not always better; balance and freshness matter.

Serving and handling

Keep oysters properly chilled and follow safe handling practices. Customers should consume raw oysters only if comfortable with the food-safety considerations.

Reservation advice

Premium oyster boxes can be seasonal. Check the current product listing for origin, grade, quantity and price.

Frequently asked questions

Which oyster brands does Crab Talk focus on?

Fanny Bay Sunseeker, Coffin Bay and selected seasonal oysters.

Are oysters available every day?

No. Availability depends on shipment and quality.

Can I reserve a box?

Yes. Order the listed quantity through the online store when the current oyster batch is available.

Can oysters be eaten raw?

Some customers eat suitable oysters raw, but raw shellfish carries food-safety risk. Keep them chilled and make an informed decision.

Order before visiting

Live and seasonal seafood can move quickly. Check the online store before making a special trip, especially if you need a particular size or quantity.
