Best cooked with ginger and scallion

Razor Clams do not need a heavy sauce. Start with a hot wok, fry sliced ginger until fragrant, add the clams, then finish with scallion as the shells open. A small splash of cooking wine or light seasoning is enough. The main thing is not to keep cooking after the meat is done.

  • Rinse the shells well before cooking.
  • Use high heat and keep the cooking quick.
  • Add scallion near the end so it stays fresh and aromatic.
  • Discard any clam that remains firmly closed after proper cooking.
