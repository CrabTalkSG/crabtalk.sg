What Crab Talk offers

If you are cooking a saucy dish, Mud Crab is usually the practical choice. If the roe is the reason for the meal, look at Yellow Roe or Red Roe. Double Shell and King Crab are more occasion-driven buys.

Mud Crab

A versatile choice for chilli crab, black pepper, steaming and bee hoon.

Yellow Roe Crab

Selected for rich yellow roe; best with cooking styles that keep the roe as the highlight.

Red Roe Crab

A roe-focused option with a different roe colour and eating profile.

Double Shell Crab

A seasonal premium crab valued for its distinctive layered shell and rich eating experience.

Alaskan King Crab

A premium centrepiece that is best reserved in advance.

How to choose the right crab

Start with the dish, number of diners and budget. A firm meat crab is usually more practical for chilli crab, while roe crabs make more sense when the roe itself is the main attraction.

Availability and pricing

Crab sizes, origins, sex, shell condition and market cost change by shipment. Crab Talk confirms the current stock directly rather than publishing a fixed promise that may be inaccurate.

Preparation for home cooking

  • Ask about washing, cleaning, cutting and packing.
  • Keep live crab cool and cook it as soon as practical.
  • For premium roe crab, use a cooking style that does not overpower the roe.

Frequently asked questions

Which crab is best for chilli crab?

Mud Crab is usually the most practical choice because its meat and shell flavour stand up well to a strong sauce.

What is the difference between Yellow Roe and Red Roe Crab?

Both are roe-focused crabs, but the roe appearance, texture and flavour can differ. Ask to see the actual stock before deciding.

Can I reserve a specific crab size?

Yes, subject to the day’s arrival. Choose an available size and quantity through the online store when listed.

Is Alaskan King Crab always available?

No. It is a premium pre-order item and availability should be confirmed in advance.
