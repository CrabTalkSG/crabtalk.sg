What Crab Talk offers

The name comes from the new shell forming beneath the old one. When the crab is at the right stage, that extra layer gives it the texture and richness that Double Shell Crab lovers look for.

Why it is different

Double Shell Crab develops a new shell beneath the old shell. This produces a distinctive texture and makes it different from an ordinary meat crab.

Who should buy it

It suits customers looking for a special crab experience rather than the most economical meat yield. First-time buyers should ask to see the actual stock and discuss the best cooking style.

Availability and handling

Because this is a condition-specific product, Crab Talk only offers it when suitable stock is available. Reserve and collect promptly.

Frequently asked questions

Is Double Shell Crab the same as soft-shell crab?

No. Double Shell Crab is a different shell condition and is normally sold as a whole premium crab.

Is it always available?

No. It depends on suitable arrivals and should be pre-ordered.

What cooking style is best?

Simple steaming or light seasoning helps preserve the distinctive eating experience.
