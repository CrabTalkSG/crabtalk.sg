How to plan the meal

Choose a suitable Mud Crab, prepare your aromatics and confirm whether sauce and mantou are available. Follow the sauce instructions and adjust to your preferred spice and sweetness.

Availability

Sauce and mantou are supporting items, not guaranteed daily products. Add them to your WhatsApp enquiry before collection.

Serving idea

Serve the chilli crab immediately with warm mantou for dipping. Add clams, prawns or a vegetable dish if you are feeding a larger group.

Frequently asked questions

Is chilli crab sauce always available?

No. It is offered when stock is available.

Can I reserve sauce and mantou together with crab?

Yes. Include all items in the same WhatsApp message.

Which crab is suitable for chilli crab?

Mud Crab is usually a practical choice because its meat works well with a strong sauce.
