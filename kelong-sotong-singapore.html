What Crab Talk offers

The 500g box is an easy freezer staple. A quick boil in salted water is our simplest recommendation, but the sotong also works well on the grill, with sambal or in a fast stir-fry.

Recommended cooking

A quick boil in salted water is one of the easiest methods. Avoid overcooking, as squid can become tough.

Storage

Keep frozen until needed and follow safe thawing practices. Do not refreeze repeatedly.

Packaging

Each box is approximately 500g. Piece count is a guide and can vary with the size of the squid.
